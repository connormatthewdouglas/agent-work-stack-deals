Read before comparing

Rankings are the cheapest among tracked providers on our check date, not across the whole market. Vendors we don't track may cost less.

GPU-hours vs wall-clock time

Prices are per GPU-hour: one GPU for one hour. Four GPUs for 10 wall-clock hours is 40 GPU-hours. Per-second billing (serverless) charges only while your code runs; hourly pods bill from start to stop, idle time included; monthly servers bill a flat price whatever you use. A faster GPU can finish the same job in fewer GPU-hours, so the lowest hourly rate isn't always the lowest cost per job.

Hardware differences

The same GPU name can mean different hardware. SXM modules link GPUs over NVLink and run at higher power limits than PCIe cards, so an H100 SXM is usually faster than an H100 PCIe, especially for multi-GPU training. Interconnect between GPUs and nodes (NVLink, InfiniBand, plain Ethernet) is rarely listed on pricing pages; treat it as unknown unless the vendor states it. VRAM decides which models fit at all. Interruptible vs on-demand

Interruptible (preemptible or spot) capacity can be stopped by the vendor mid-job and is usually cheaper; on-demand runs until you stop it. Interruptible (preemptible) rates listed here: SaladCloud RTX 4090. All other rates are on-demand: the vendor doesn't publish preemption for them.

What is the cheapest RTX 4090 per hour among tracked providers?

What does a full month of RTX 4090 cost?

What is the difference between RunPod Community Cloud and Secure Cloud?

Both are per-hour pods. Community Cloud runs on vetted third-party hosts and costs less; Secure Cloud runs in RunPod's partner data centers. Pick Secure Cloud for sensitive data or stricter uptime needs.

Why are dedicated servers listed with a flat price?

Dedicated GPU servers bill a fixed monthly price whatever your usage, so they only pay off when the GPU runs most of the month.

What isn't included in these prices?

Storage, data egress, extra vCPU/RAM and taxes. Prices are list or 'from' prices; confirm on the vendor page before you rent.

How are these prices verified?

Each row quotes the vendor's own pricing page and shows the date it was checked. Offers without a verified price aren't listed, and referral links don't change the order.
