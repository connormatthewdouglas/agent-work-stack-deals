What is the difference between RunPod Community Cloud and Secure Cloud?

Both are per-hour pods. Community Cloud runs on vetted third-party hosts and is cheaper; Secure Cloud runs in RunPod's partner data centers. Pick Secure Cloud for sensitive data or stricter uptime needs.

Why are dedicated servers listed with a flat price?

Dedicated GPU servers bill a fixed monthly price whatever your usage, so they only pay off when the GPU runs most of the month.
