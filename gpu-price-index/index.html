Method

Per GPU model family (SXM/PCIe/NVLink variants grouped), take each tracked provider's cheapest listed on-demand per-GPU hourly rate; publish the median across providers, plus min, max and provider count. Families with fewer than 2 providers are omitted. Interruptible, community, marketplace, reserved and monthly tiers are excluded. Scope: tracked providers only, not the whole market. Daily history is rebuilt from the price-history change points.
