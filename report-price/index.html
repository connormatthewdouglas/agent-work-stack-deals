Report a wrong GPU price

Saw a different price on a vendor's page than the one Lantern Works shows? Tell us and it will be re-checked by hand against the vendor's own page. Nothing changes in the feed until it is verified there (see how prices are checked).

This form doesn't send anything by itself: it opens a prefilled issue on GitHub (a free GitHub account is needed) that you review and submit. Reports are public, so don't include account or billing details.

What a report contains

  • Vendor, GPU (with VRAM and tier) and the feed deal_id
  • Shown price: our hourly_equiv_usd for that row, read from the live feed
  • Observed price and the source URL where you saw it, plus when you checked

Agents and scripts can open the same report directly: https://github.com/connormatthewdouglas/agent-work-stack-deals/issues/new?template=wrong-price.yml&vendor=…&gpu=…&shown_price=…&observed_price=…&source_url=…

Verified-on dates are each offer's own verified_at; offers without one are marked unverified. Affiliate links are marked and use rel="sponsored"; public/non-affiliate badges mark free tiers and vendor-published coupons (no invented codes).
