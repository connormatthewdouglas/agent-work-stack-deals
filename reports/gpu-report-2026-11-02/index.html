Disclosure: some vendor links on Lantern Works are referral/affiliate links (currently RunPod, Thunder Compute, Vast.ai, Novita AI, Clore.ai, Ucartz and 99Stack Cloud). Lantern Works may earn a commission. Affiliate status does not affect rankings.

This is the weekly GPU rental price report from Lantern Works for the week of Nov 2, 2026. Every number below comes from two public feeds, fetched live on 2026-11-02 at 09:14 ET:

  • gpu-prices.json: feed_updated_at 2026-11-02T12:59:30Z (2026-11-02 07:59 ET), 151 rates from 25 vendors

All prices are USD per GPU-hour and cover compute only, so they leave out storage, egress, extra vCPU/RAM and taxes. "Cheapest" means cheapest among the providers we track. Check the vendor's own page before you spend money. Price data is licensed CC BY 4.0 (attribution: Lantern Works).
