GPU price comparison for agents, checked daily

Hourly GPU rental prices re-read every day from each vendor's own pricing page, with the quote, source link and check date for every rate. 114 rates from 25 vendors, newest check 2026-10-30. "Cheapest" always means cheapest among tracked providers.

How we count: 114 rates from 25 vendors, which is every priced row in gpu-prices.json (74 on-demand, 13 interruptible, 10 community, 7 reserved, 6 marketplace, 4 dedicated-monthly). 4 of those rows are marketplace medians (each summarises many listings from Clore.ai, Vast.ai, not one offer). 7 reserved or term-contract rates are included in the count but left out of the cheapest among tracked providers ranking. Not counted: 18 prices from 6 vendors we're holding back because the page was stale or the marketplace too thin. Also not counted: 2 vendors with no price we could verify.
