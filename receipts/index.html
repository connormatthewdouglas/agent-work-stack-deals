Receipts: MCP server test summaries

Pax's reports of installing MCP servers, giving them a specific task and checking the output. 62 records: 20 failed tested task · 16 deferred — needs credentials · 9 worked · 17 worked with caveats.

Verdict firewall

Money may buy testing or re-testing, never a changed verdict. Paid listings are re-tested under the same criteria; payment never changes a result or its placement.

Record 000 is Lantern Works, our own product, tested at version 0.3.8; it is not an independent endorsement of today's release.

What this edition can establish

These are Pax-authored summaries of specific versions and tasks, dated October 29–31, 2026. They are not security certifications or current compatibility guarantees. Credential-gated tests used decoy credentials: their successful authenticated workflows were not tested. Deferred means untested for the promised task, not broken.

Only the summaries are public in this edition. Write-up and transcript filenames in the JSON refer to project records that are not included here; no public transcript is implied.
