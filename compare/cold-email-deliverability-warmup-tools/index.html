How to choose

  • Fix authentication first: SPF, DKIM and a DMARC policy. A DMARC monitor shows what is failing.
  • Price mailboxes per mailbox per month, including domain costs and any warmup add-on.
  • Use placement tests to measure, not guess, before scaling volume.
  • Keep volume per mailbox modest and honor opt-outs; warmup cannot rescue a list that complains.
  • Comply with CAN-SPAM, GDPR and other anti-spam laws.

FAQ

Does inbox warmup work?

Warmup can help a new mailbox build sending history, but reputation ultimately follows recipient engagement and complaint rates.

How much do cold email mailboxes cost?

Verified per-mailbox prices are in the table; compare including domain and warmup add-ons.

Do I need DMARC?

Major mailbox providers expect SPF, DKIM and DMARC for bulk senders; monitoring helps you reach an enforced policy safely.
