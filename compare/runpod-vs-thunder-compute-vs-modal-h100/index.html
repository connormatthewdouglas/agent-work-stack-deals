FAQ

Which is cheapest for an H100: RunPod, Thunder Compute or Modal?

When is Modal cheaper than an hourly H100?

When your GPU sits idle a lot. Modal charges per second only while code runs, so a spiky inference workload can cost less even at a higher listed rate. For a GPU busy most of the hour, the lower hourly rate wins.

What isn't included?

Storage, extra CPU/RAM, egress and taxes. Confirm on each vendor page before you rent.
