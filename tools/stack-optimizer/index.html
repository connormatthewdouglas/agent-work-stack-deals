Agent Stack Optimizer

Tell it what your agent needs each month (pages to scrape, proxy traffic, emails, searches, GPU hours) and it picks the cheapest suitable plan per need, cheapest among tracked providers (vendors we don't track may cost less). Every line shows the vendor quote, its source page, when we checked it, and the assumptions. If a vendor doesn't publish a number, the line says unknown; nothing is guessed. Referral links never change the order.

How it's calculated

  • Each need is priced on its own: a plan's monthly price if your volume fits what it includes, plus published overage (rounded up to whole packs), else the plan doesn't qualify.
  • Credit-based plans convert your quantity with the vendor's published rate (e.g. 5 credits per JS-rendered page).
  • Monthly list prices before tax; free tiers count when your volume fits. Plans that share one credit pool across needs aren't combined.
  • Plans marked Manual signup only need a human to sign up; agents shouldn't automate it.
  • Prices are re-fetched from each source page daily; confirm on the vendor page before you spend.
