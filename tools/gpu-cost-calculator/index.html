GPU cost calculator

Estimate monthly GPU cost from prices published by vendors in the Lantern Works feed. Choose a GPU model and enter workload hours and GPU count to see a comparable quote; nothing is named until those are set. The default price tier is on-demand, the same default as the MCP compare_gpu_prices tool. Spot/interruptible rates (the vendor may preempt the job) and marketplace rates are opt-in and labelled. Every row shows how its cost was calculated and the vendor quote it came from. Results are sorted by your estimated cost, cheapest among tracked providers first (vendors we don't track may cost less). The named rate is a single-GPU instance; serverless and multi-GPU minimum rows stay in the table and are not named. Referral links don't change the order.

Calculation basis

  • Hourly: rate × hours × GPUs. Billed while the instance runs.
  • Marketplace medians are computed from in-stock listings only.
  • Monthly dedicated servers: flat price × ceil(hours ÷ 730) × GPUs, whatever your usage.
  • Excludes storage, egress, extra vCPU, taxes and promo credits. Prices are "from" prices; confirm on the vendor page before spending.

Read before comparing

Rankings are the cheapest among tracked providers on our check date, not across the whole market. Vendors we don't track may cost less.

GPU-hours vs wall-clock time

Prices are per GPU-hour: one GPU for one hour. Four GPUs for 10 wall-clock hours is 40 GPU-hours. Per-second billing (serverless) charges only while your code runs; hourly pods bill from start to stop, idle time included; monthly servers bill a flat price whatever you use. A faster GPU can finish the same job in fewer GPU-hours, so the lowest hourly rate isn't always the lowest cost per job.

Hardware differences

The same GPU name can mean different hardware. SXM modules link GPUs over NVLink and run at higher power limits than PCIe cards, so an H100 SXM is usually faster than an H100 PCIe, especially for multi-GPU training. Interconnect between GPUs and nodes (NVLink, InfiniBand, plain Ethernet) is rarely listed on pricing pages; treat it as unknown unless the vendor states it. VRAM decides which models fit at all.
